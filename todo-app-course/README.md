# Build & Deploy Your First App — a $0, beginner-friendly course

Go from **never having coded** to a **real to-do app that's live on the internet** — for free,
with a friendly tutor named **Ada** guiding you the whole way, right here inside Claude Code.

You'll build a to-do list app with Next.js and Claude Code, then put it online with GitHub and
Vercel. No prior experience needed. **Total cost: $0.** (Free GitHub account, free Vercel Hobby
plan, and your to-do data lives in your own browser — no database, nothing that ever bills you.)

> **Meet Ada, your tutor.** Throughout this course, Claude teaches as a friendly guide named
> **Ada** — she greets you, explains each step, runs commands for you, checks your work, and
> tracks your progress. The name is a nod to *Ada Lovelace*, often called the world's first
> programmer. When you open this folder, Ada introduces herself and offers to begin Lesson 1.

---

## What you'll be able to do by the end

- Have a working to-do app **live at your own web address**, shareable with anyone.
- Understand, in plain English, what GitHub, Vercel, and Next.js are and how they fit together.
- Make a change, push it, and watch your live site update automatically.

## How to start

### In Claude Code
1. Open this `todo-app-course` folder in Claude Code.
2. Ada greets you automatically. Just say **"I'm ready"** or type **`/lesson`** to begin.

### In Claude Cowork
1. Unzip and open this folder.
2. Say hello — Ada will read where you are and get you started. All the commands below also work
   as plain English (e.g. "show me my progress", "give me a hint", "next lesson").

You need nothing installed in advance. This course assumes a **recent Mac** — in Lesson 1.2 Ada
checks your tools (Node.js, Git) and walks you through installing anything that's missing, step by
step (see `reference/installing-on-mac.md`). No prior setup required.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/course` | Show the course map, your progress bar, and badges. |
| `/lesson` | Start or continue from where you left off. |
| `/skip [id]` | Jump to a specific lesson, e.g. `/skip 4.1`. |
| `/progress` | See how far you've come. |
| `/hint` | Get a nudge on the current task (ask again for a bigger hint). |
| `/check` | Have Ada check your work and mark it complete when it's right. |
| `/exit` | Wrap up and save your progress. |

In Cowork, just say the plain-English version of any of these.

## The course at a glance

1. **Welcome & Setup** — meet your tools; make sure everything works.
2. **GitHub** — a free online home for your code.
3. **Vercel** — free hosting that puts your app on the web.
4. **Create & Deploy** — get a *live URL* fast, even before the app is finished.
5. **Build the To-Do App** — add, complete, delete, and save your tasks.
6. **Ship & Iterate** — verify it live, then feel the edit → push → auto-update loop.

## Your progress is saved

Everything lives in `progress.json`. Copy it to back up. Delete it to start completely over —
the course resets cleanly. You can also open it to see exactly where you are.

Nothing in this course needs an account you have to pay for, a server, or an internet connection
beyond the free accounts you'll create. Ready when you are. 💚
