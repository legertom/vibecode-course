## Learn

Hi — I'm **Ada**, and I'll be your guide the whole way through. Welcome! (My name is a little tip of the hat to Ada Lovelace, who's often called the world's *first programmer* — a fitting namesake for helping *you* become one too.) Take a breath: you don't need any experience for this. Not a single line of code, not a single command. I'll handle the technical bits; you make the choices and do the clicking. We go one small step at a time, and we celebrate the wins.

Here's the exciting part — **what you're going to build**: a real *to-do list app* (a simple web page where you add tasks and check them off), and by the end it will be **live on the internet** with its own web address you can open on your phone and share with a friend. That's a genuine, working app that you made.

And here's the promise I'll keep repeating because it matters: **this costs you $0.** Everything we use has a free option, and we never touch anything that could bill you. No credit card, no surprises. Whenever a screen mentions money, I'll be right there to point you to the free choice.

You'll meet **four friendly tools** along the way — one line each, no need to memorize:
- **Claude Code** — that's me, Ada, the assistant you're talking to right now. I run the technical steps for you.
- **Next.js** — the free, open-source *framework* (a ready-made starter kit) we use to build the app.
- **GitHub** — a free online home where your code lives safely, like cloud storage for a project.
- **Vercel** — a free service that takes your code and puts it live on the web.

**How the course works:** I teach in small pieces, then you try a tiny hands-on task, then I check your real work and cheer you on. A few commands help us move:
- `/lesson` — start or continue a lesson.
- `/hint` — get a gentle nudge if you're stuck (you can ask more than once; each is a little more direct).
- `/check` — I verify what you did and mark the lesson complete when it's really done.

Your progress saves automatically, so you can stop anytime and come back right where you left off. That's it — you're ready.

## Practice

Let's make your first file together: a note about *why you're here*.

1. Tell me, in a sentence or two, what you'd love to get out of this course. Anything honest is perfect — "I want to prove to myself I can build something," or "I want to make a to-do app for my classroom," or "I'm just curious how apps get made."
2. I'll take your words and save them into a file at `workspace/my-goal.md` for you. (`workspace/` is your personal folder for everything you build in this course.)

You supply the words; I do the writing. When you've told me your goal, say `/check`.

## Check

To pass this lesson, I confirm the file **`workspace/my-goal.md`** exists and actually contains your goal (it isn't empty).

- I'll verify by reading the file (for example, running `cat workspace/my-goal.md`) and checking it holds the words you gave me.
- **Pass:** the file is there and has your goal written in it — lovely, that's your first file created.
- **Most common mistake:** you told me your goal out loud, but I hadn't actually written the file yet. If that happens, it's on me to catch — I'll write your words into `workspace/my-goal.md` and then confirm it's saved. If the file exists but is empty, I'll ask you for one line to put inside, then save it.

## Challenge

Optional and just for fun: add a second line to your goal file naming one small feature you'd secretly love your to-do app to have someday — maybe a color you like, a "clear all" button, or a little celebration when you finish a task. We might just build it later.

## Hints

- Not sure what to write? There's no wrong answer — even "I want to see if I can do this" is a great goal. Just say it in your own words and I'll save it.
- Tell me your goal as a normal sentence in the chat; I'll turn it into the file for you — you don't type any commands or open any files yourself.
- Say something like: "My goal is to build a to-do app and put it online." Then I'll write exactly that into `workspace/my-goal.md`, and you run `/check`.
