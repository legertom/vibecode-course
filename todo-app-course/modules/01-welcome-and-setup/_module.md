# Module 1 — Welcome & Setup

**Goal:** Get comfortable and get your toolbox ready. By the end of this module you'll know exactly what you're building, you'll have the free tools your computer needs, and you'll feel at home typing (or watching Ada type) a few simple commands. No experience needed, and every part of this course is free — $0, start to finish.

## Lessons

- **1.1 Welcome** — Meet your project (a real to-do list app, live on the internet), the $0 promise, how the course works, and the four friendly tools you'll use.
- **1.2 Toolbox check** — Confirm your computer has Node.js and Git (the two behind-the-scenes helpers), and install them together if anything's missing.
- **1.3 Terminal & folders 101** — Learn the three gentle basics — where am I, what's here, how do I move — and see your very first files. Earns the **setup-complete** badge.

## What you'll walk away with

A computer that's fully ready to build and ship an app, a clear picture of the journey ahead, and your first written goal saved in your own workspace. You'll have proven your tools work — and you'll know nothing here will ever cost you a cent.
