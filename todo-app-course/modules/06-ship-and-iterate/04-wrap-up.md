## Learn

Take a breath and look at what you did. You started with nothing — maybe you'd never coded,
never used a terminal, never deployed anything — and now there's a **real to-do app, live on
the internet, at your own web address**, that you can share with anyone.

Here's everything you picked up along the way:

- **Talking to Claude Code** to build software in plain English.
- **GitHub** — a free online home for your code, with a full history you can always look back
  through.
- **Vercel** — free Hobby hosting that puts your app on the web.
- **The edit → push → deploy loop** — change your code, push it, watch it go live
  automatically. The exact loop professional developers use every day.

And the whole thing cost **$0**, on purpose:

- GitHub — free.
- Vercel Hobby plan — free (yes, even with your Clever work email).
- Next.js — free and open source.
- Your tasks save in the browser's **localStorage** instead of a paid database — so there's
  nothing running up a bill, ever.

That's not a toy. That's the real workflow, at real-developer scale, for free.

**Optional next steps — beyond this course, and still free if you explore:**
- **A custom domain** — point a web address you like at your app. (Vercel lets you connect
  one; a domain name itself is a small yearly cost from a registrar, but connecting it to
  Vercel is free.)
- **A free database** — if you ever want your tasks to **sync across devices** (so your phone
  and laptop share one list), you could later add a free-tier database. That's a bigger topic
  for another day — this course intentionally kept things free and simple with localStorage.

These are just ideas for later. You are *done* — and you shipped something real.

## Practice

Let's make it a keepsake. Tell Ada:

1. **What you built**, in your own words — even just "a to-do app that's live on the internet."
2. **Your live URL** — paste the web address so you can share it and Ada can confirm it's up.
3. **One thing you learned** in this course, and/or **one optional next step** you might try
   someday.

Ada will save this — your live URL and a line about what you built — into a short keepsake file
(`workspace/my-goal.md` or a new `workspace/SHIPPED.md`) so it's there whenever you want to look
back or show someone.

## Check

Say `/check` when you've shared your reflection.

**Pass looks like:**
- Ada fetches your pasted live URL and confirms it responds **HTTP 200** — your app is truly
  live.
- You've said, in your own words, one thing you learned and/or one optional next step.

When both are true, Ada records your keepsake, awards the **`shipped-it`** badge, and marks the
whole course **completed**. This is the finish line — celebrate it. You built and shipped a real
app, from zero, for free.

**Common mistakes Ada will gently help with:**
- *No live URL handy* — grab it from your Vercel project or your browser's address bar on the
  live site. Ada can help you find it.
- *The URL doesn't respond* — it may be an old or mistyped address; Ada will re-check and help
  you get the right one, since your app is deployed.

## Challenge

Share your live URL with one person — a coworker at Clever, a friend, a family member — and
watch them add a task on their own device. Their tasks stay on their device (that's
localStorage again), but the joy of saying *"I built this and it's live"* is all yours. If you
want to keep tinkering, the `playground/` folder is a free, no-pressure place to experiment.
