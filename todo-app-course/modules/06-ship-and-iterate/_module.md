## Module 6 — Ship, Verify & Iterate

You built a working to-do app. Now let's get the finished version onto the internet, make
sure it really works out there, and feel the loop that every developer lives in: make a
change, send it up, watch it go live — automatically.

This is the fun part. Nothing new to install, nothing to pay for. Your GitHub account is
free, your Vercel Hobby hosting is free, and your app saves tasks right in the browser, so
there's no database and nothing that ever bills you. Total cost, still $0.

### Lessons in this module

- **6.1 — Commit & push; watch it deploy.** Save a labeled snapshot of your app, send it to
  GitHub, and watch Vercel put the real to-do app live.
- **6.2 — Verify the live app.** Open your live web address on your computer *and* your phone,
  add a task, refresh, and confirm it sticks.
- **6.3 — One small change → push → live.** Make a tiny personal edit, push it, and watch it
  appear on your live site all by itself.
- **6.4 — Wrap-up.** Celebrate what you built, see how it all stayed free, and pick an
  optional next step if you want one.

### What you'll walk away with

A real to-do app **live at your own web address**, shareable with anyone — plus the confidence
to change it, push it, and watch the internet update itself. You'll have shipped a real app.
