# Module 2 — GitHub: your code's home

Welcome to Module 2! In the last module you got your tools ready and learned your
way around the terminal. Now we'll give your code a safe, free home on the internet.

That home is called **GitHub** — a website where your project lives online. It backs
up your work, remembers every change you make (so you can always undo), and later it's
the place your live website reads from when it publishes your app. Think of it as the
foundation everything else in this course sits on.

Everything here is **free**. Creating a GitHub account costs $0, and it stays $0 for
everything we do in this course. No credit card, no surprises.

## What you'll do in this module

- **2.1 — What GitHub is and why** · Understand, in plain words, why we're using GitHub:
  free backup, a full history of your changes, and the source your live app deploys from.
- **2.2 — Create your free GitHub account** · Sign up at github.com and verify your email
  (a work email or a personal email — both are fine).
- **2.3 — Connect your computer to GitHub** · Introduce your computer to GitHub using a
  friendly tool called the GitHub CLI, and set your name and email so your work is
  labeled as yours. Earns the **github-ready** badge.

## What you'll walk away with

A real GitHub account that exists on the web, and a computer that can talk to it securely —
ready to send your app's code up to the cloud in later modules. That's the launch pad for
your very first live website.
