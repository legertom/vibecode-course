## Learn

Time to create your Vercel account. This is a browser step — you'll do the clicking, and Ada will
walk you through each part and help if anything looks confusing.

Two things to know before you start, so nothing surprises you:

**1. Use your Clever work email — the one ending in `clever.com`.** For your work account, Vercel
should be tied to your company email. (This is different from GitHub, where a personal email was
fine.) Have your `clever.com` email open and ready.

**2. Vercel signs you in with a "magic link."** Instead of making you invent a password, Vercel
emails you a link; you click it, and you're in. So keep your email inbox handy — you'll pop over to
it for a second.

And the reassurance worth repeating: **using a work email does not make this cost money.** You'll
choose the free **Hobby** plan, and it stays $0. There's no card to enter. If you ever see a screen
offering a paid or "Pro" plan, that's optional — the free Hobby plan is all this course needs.

At the end you'll land on your Vercel **dashboard** — your account's home screen. It'll look empty,
which is exactly right; you have no projects yet. That's next module.

## Practice

Let's make the account. Follow these steps — Ada is right here if any screen looks different from
the description (button wording changes sometimes; go by what the button is *for*).

1. In your web browser, go to **vercel.com**.
2. Find the option to **sign up** (often a "Sign Up" button near the top-right).
3. When asked how you want to sign up, choose the option to **continue with email** (not the
   GitHub/Google buttons — we'll connect GitHub separately in the next lesson).
4. Enter your **Clever work email** (your `clever.com` address) and submit it.
5. Open your email inbox, find the message from Vercel, and **click the sign-in link** inside it.
   (If you don't see it in a minute, check spam/junk.)
6. Back on Vercel, if it asks you to pick a plan, choose the **free Hobby plan**. If it asks for
   your name or a username, fill those in — no card is required.
7. You'll arrive at your **dashboard**. It'll be empty (no projects yet) — perfect.

When you're done, tell Ada and paste (or describe) two things from your account/dashboard:
- the **email** shown on your account (it should end in `clever.com`), and
- that your plan shows as **Hobby**.

## Check

Because Ada can't see your browser, Ada confirms this from what you share.

**Pass when:** you're signed in and on your dashboard, your account email ends in **`clever.com`**,
and your plan reads **Hobby**. Ada will also be able to confirm everything later, automatically,
the first time your app deploys — so if you're mostly sure, that's fine; we'll double-check then.

**Most common mistakes Ada will help you fix:**
- **Used a personal email instead of your work email.** For the work account we want your
  `clever.com` address. Ada will help you sign out and sign up again with the right email (or, if
  Vercel allows it, add/switch the email in your account settings).
- **Accidentally landed on a paid or "Pro" plan.** No harm done — Ada will help you switch back to
  the free **Hobby** plan (usually in your account or team settings, under the plan/billing area).
  You won't be charged; there's no card on file.
- **Didn't get the magic link.** Wait a minute, check spam, and if needed re-request it from the
  same email screen. Ada can talk you through it.

To confirm what to paste, Ada may ask: "What email is shown on your Vercel account, and what does
your plan say?"

## Challenge

Peek at your (empty) dashboard and find where your account settings live (look for your avatar or
name, usually top-right). Locate the section that shows your **plan**. Just noticing where "Hobby"
is displayed now means you'll know exactly where to look if you ever want to confirm you're still on
the free plan.

## Hints

- Start at **vercel.com** and look for the sign-up option; when it offers ways to sign up, pick the
  plain **email** one rather than the GitHub or Google buttons.
- Use your **clever.com** work email. Vercel won't send a password — it emails you a link to click.
  Check your inbox (and spam) for a message from Vercel and click the link inside.
- If a plan screen appears, choose **Hobby** (the free one). No card is needed. You'll then land on
  an empty dashboard — that's the finish line. Tell Ada your account email and that it says "Hobby".
