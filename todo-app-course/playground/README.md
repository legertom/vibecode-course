# 🛝 Playground — your no-pressure sandbox

This is your space to **experiment freely**. Nothing here is graded, nothing here can break the
course, and there's no "wrong." If you're curious about something, try it here.

Ideas for things to try (ask Ada anytime):
- "Ada, can you show me what a button looks like in code?"
- "Make me a tiny web page that says hello and my name."
- "What happens if I change this color?"
- "Explain what this line of code does."

Want to poke at your real app instead? That lives in `../workspace/todo-app/` once you build it
in Module 4 — but do your risky experiments *here* first if you're nervous. You can always
delete anything in this folder; it won't affect your course progress or your app.

Play is how you learn. Have fun. 💚
